# Editorial policy

Korean Tech Wire seeks primary or near-primary Korean-language information that can give an English-language technology journalist an information advantage.

Prioritise concrete, fresh reporting or disclosures about semiconductors, memory, foundries, packaging, displays, consumer hardware, component supply chains, relevant batteries, and Korean technology companies. Valuable signals include production changes, yields, capacity, contracts, pricing, product/certification identifiers, roadmaps, shipment quantities, and regulatory filings.

Do not equate a new article with an alert. Future editorial processing should separately judge freshness, originality, authority, beat relevance, specificity, Western propagation, magnitude, and extraction confidence.

Suppress routine promotions, stock-market boilerplate, generic AI commentary, appointments, CSR, telecom-plan offers, celebrity/crypto material, recycled rumours, and stories that mention a target company without a material technology fact. Preserve the Korean original for review; uncertain terminology, names, Korean numeric units (억, 조, 만), and technical labels must not be silently normalised.
